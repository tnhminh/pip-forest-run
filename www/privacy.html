What we collect ourselves

Nothing. We do not run servers for the game and we do not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or account. Your progress (acorns, outfits, missions, settings, best score) is stored only on your device. Uninstalling the game or using "Reset progress" in Settings deletes it.

Consent and your choices

  • If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy choices.
  • On iOS the game asks for permission before using your device's advertising identifier. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in system Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Daily reminder notifications are optional and only scheduled on your device. You can turn them off in your phone's notification settings.
  • Buying "No Ads" or the Starter Pack turns off full-screen ads. Rewarded ads only play when you tap a button that asks for one.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through a third-party service in the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Security and retention

Game progress stays on your device until you delete it. Data processed by the services above is kept according to their own policies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA) you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Because we hold no personal data on our side, requests about advertising or purchase data are best sent to Google or RevenueCat directly; we are happy to help you do that at [CONTACT EMAIL].
